Privacy Notice

SP Ads API Internal Tool · Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Notice explains how the SP Ads API Internal Tool (the “Application”), operated by an independent e-commerce seller, handles information when connecting to Amazon Ads. The Application is intended for internal advertising management and reporting and is not offered for sale to third parties.

1. Information we process

With the advertiser’s authorization, the Application may process Amazon Ads account and profile identifiers, campaign settings, budgets, bids, targeting information, advertised product identifiers, performance metrics, and advertising reports. It also processes Login with Amazon authorization credentials, including access and refresh tokens, solely to maintain the authorized connection.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to retrieve advertising reports, monitor advertising performance, support internal analysis, and manage or optimize authorized Sponsored Products advertising activity. The Application does not sell personal information or Amazon Ads data and does not use it for unrelated advertising or profiling.

3. Legal basis and authorization

Amazon Ads data is accessed only after an authorized advertiser grants consent through Login with Amazon. The advertiser may revoke that authorization through the applicable Amazon account or advertising settings.

4. Storage and security

Credentials and advertising data are access-controlled and protected using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Credentials are not committed to public source-code repositories. Access is limited to persons who need it to operate the Application.

5. Sharing

Information is not sold or rented. It may be processed by infrastructure or service providers only as necessary to operate the Application, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations, or disclosed when required by law.

6. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described above, to comply with applicable requirements, or until the advertiser withdraws authorization. When no longer required, it is deleted or anonymized. Requests to access or delete information may be submitted using the contact details below.

7. International processing

Depending on the location of the Application’s infrastructure and service providers, information may be processed outside the advertiser’s country or region, with safeguards appropriate to applicable law.

8. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when the Application or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are made.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact 19370692130@163.com.
